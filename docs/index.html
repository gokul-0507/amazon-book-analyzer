About This Data & Analytical Limits

Source & Scope

This explorer utilizes the Kaggle Amazon Top 50 Bestselling Books (2009–2019) dataset originally curated by Sohier Dane and Vijay Choudhary. It contains strictly 550 annual bestseller listings across 11 calendar years.

Category Granularity

Amazon’s taxonomy in this dataset is restricted strictly to two macro genres: Fiction and Non Fiction. Subcategories such as memoirs, graphic novels, culinary, or business texts are consolidated within these two designations.

What This Data Cannot Answer

Customer reviews represent historical snapshots at peak listing time rather than cumulative daily reads. Zero-dollar price points represent temporary Kindle promotions or complimentary print companion editions. Unit sales volumes and royalty splits are not disclosed by Amazon.
