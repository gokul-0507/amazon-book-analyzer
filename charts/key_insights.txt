Across all 550 rankings between 2009 and 2019, Non Fiction secured the majority share of the bestseller lists at 56.4% versus 43.6% for Fiction, peaking in 2015 at 66%. Author Jeff Kinney dominated individual creator output with 12 distinct bestselling titles. List turnover remained dynamic, introducing an average of 29.8 brand-new titles per year after 2009. Longevity analysis reveals that Non Fiction titles enjoyed slightly longer shelf lives (1.63 years on average) compared to Fiction (1.53 years), led by 'Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association, 6th Edition' which charted for a record 10 consecutive years. The most reviewed work was 'Where the Crawdads Sing' with 87,841 customer reviews and an average rating of 4.8. Finally, empirical correlation between book price and user ratings was negligible (Pearson r = -0.12), demonstrating that higher pricing (averaging $13.39 overall) neither detracted from nor boosted reader satisfaction.
